RAIV: Robotics, AI & Vision

RAIV continues the Computer Vision and Robotics (CVR) group at Bahria University Lahore Campus and builds on work begun in the VIG & Robotics centre at COMSATS Lahore. We work on problems where a machine must perceive, decide and move.

Robot path planning

Optimal and collision-free paths for non-holonomic mobile robots: RRT*-AB, memory-efficient A*, coverage planning, and B-spline smoothing. Funded by an HEC-SRGP grant.

Deep reinforcement learning for navigation

Collision avoidance for robots in unknown environments, published in PLOS ONE (2025).

Medical image analysis

Alzheimer's and mild cognitive impairment detection, tumour diagnosis, and retinal exudate detection with deep and transfer learning.

Video forensics & security

Forged-video and duplicate-frame detection, and deepfake prevention using steganography GANs.

Vision & applied AI

Hand-pose recognition, plant-disease detection, elderly-care robots, and churn prediction.
